## AI-Powered NF Research: Accelerate Your Scientific Discoveries

Are you an NF researcher looking to leverage AI to accelerate your work? 
Join our hands-on workshop where you'll learn to harness Claude for Desktop—the leading AI desktop client—specifically for NF scientific research.
We'll guide you through connecting to the beta Model Context Protocol (MCP) server developed by the NF-OSI, unlocking AI-assisted access to specialized tools and resources from the NF community and Synapse.


### Workshop Agenda

- Setting Up: Connecting to NF resources through the MCP server
- Summary of Tools & Resources Provided:
  - Semantically queryable knowledgebase of 300+ indexed NF publications
  - Access to the Tools Central database
  - Access to NF Data Catalog
  - Table queries for easy question-answering and exploratory analysis
  - Access to the NF data model

### Practical Demonstrations

- For Scientists: Discover research tools for your studies
- For Data Users: Conducting exploratory queries of Synapse tables and comparing datasets
- For Data Contributors: Querying the NF data model to share your data more effectively

### Questions & Discussion: Open forum for addressing specific questions and exploring additional use cases

Leave empowered with practical skills to supercharge your research and accelerate scientific discoveries in the NF field!
