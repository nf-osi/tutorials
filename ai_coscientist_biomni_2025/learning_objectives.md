# AI Co-Scientist Workshop Learning Objectives

## Primary Learning Objectives

### 1. AI Tool Awareness and Understanding
- Identify key AI co-scientist tools available for biomedical research
- Understand capabilities and limitations of current AI research platforms
- Distinguish between specialized biomedical AI tools and general AI assistants

### 2. Biomni Platform Proficiency
- Navigate the Biomni interface effectively
- Execute literature searches and data queries using AI assistance
- Interpret AI-generated research summaries and recommendations

### 3. Synapse Integration and Data Management
- Understand how AI tools integrate with Synapse data repositories
- Recognize opportunities for contributing research data to benefit the community
- Identify valuable datasets in Synapse for AI-assisted analysis and reuse

### 4. Practical Research Applications
- Apply AI tools to common research tasks (literature review, data analysis, hypothesis generation)
- Evaluate AI-generated outputs for accuracy and relevance
- Integrate AI assistance into existing research workflows

### 5. Critical Assessment Skills
- Assess the reliability and limitations of AI-generated research insights
- Identify appropriate use cases for AI co-scientist tools
- Recognize potential biases and ethical considerations in AI-assisted research

## Secondary Learning Objectives

### 6. Community and Collaboration
- Connect with other researchers using AI tools in biomedical research
- Share experiences and best practices for AI tool adoption
- Understand collaborative opportunities enabled by AI platforms

### 7. Future Research Planning
- Develop strategies for implementing AI tools in ongoing research projects
- Identify barriers to adoption and potential solutions
- Plan for continuous learning and adaptation as AI tools evolve

### 8. Data Sharing and Open Science
- Appreciate the value of data sharing for AI tool development
- Understand how contributing data enhances AI capabilities for the entire research community
- Recognize the mutual benefits of open science practices in the AI era

## Skill-Based Outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:
- [ ] Demonstrate basic proficiency with at least one AI co-scientist platform
- [ ] Formulate effective queries for AI-assisted literature searches
- [ ] Critically evaluate AI-generated research summaries
- [ ] Identify 2-3 specific ways AI tools could enhance their current research
- [ ] Explain the connection between data sharing and AI tool effectiveness
- [ ] Articulate both benefits and limitations of AI in scientific research

## Assessment Methods

### Immediate (During Workshop)
- Hands-on exercises with AI platforms
- Group discussions and peer feedback
- Q&A sessions with expert facilitators

### Short-term (Post-Workshop Survey)
- Self-reported confidence levels
- Implementation intentions
- Identified barriers and facilitators

### Long-term (Follow-up Studies)
- Actual tool adoption and usage patterns
- Research productivity metrics
- Data contribution and reuse behaviors
- Collaboration network changes
